# Liva Dental

FICTIONAL brand (portfolio concept project). Dental clinic, South Austin, Texas.

## Story
Dr. Liva Moreno opened the clinic in South Austin in 2014, after ten years in a big dental chain where patients were rushed. Four chairs, two dentists, three hygienists. Known for gentle care with nervous patients.

## Mission
Honest dentistry with clear prices, so nobody has to fear the dentist or the bill.

## Values
We only treat what needs treating; written price before we start; extra time for nervous patients.
